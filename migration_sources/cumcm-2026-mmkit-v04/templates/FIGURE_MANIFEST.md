## Status values

- `DRAFT`: candidate figure, not yet paper-ready.
- `FINAL`: visually checked and claim-linked.
- `APPENDIX`: useful evidence but not main-text priority.
- `DROP`: redundant or decorative; exclude from final paper.

## Rule

A main-text figure cannot be marked `FINAL` unless its claim, source data, generation script, and interpretation are all non-empty.
